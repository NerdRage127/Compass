How to Use
  • Allow location access when prompted
  • Hold your device flat (parallel to the ground)
  • The compass rose rotates to always point north
  • The white needle always points to true north
  • The red needle points to Destination 1 (if selected)
  • The blue needle points to Destination 2 (if selected)
  • Distance shown in miles with estimated driving time
  • Select from preset locations or enter custom coordinates
  • For custom locations: Right-click on Google Maps and select coordinates to copy
